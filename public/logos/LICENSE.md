# Lab logos

The SVG files in this folder are taken from
[`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg) v1.98.0
(<https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons>), published under the MIT License.
They were copied unmodified apart from removing the inline `width`/`height`/`style`
attributes and, for single-colour marks, replacing `currentColor` with `#000` so they render
as standalone images (the dashboard inverts them in dark mode).
